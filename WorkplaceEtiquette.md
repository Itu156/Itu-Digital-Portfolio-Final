## 💬 Reflection — STAR Technique
**Situation:** Early in our project, team coordination issues caused missed deadlines.  
**Task:** Re-establish professional discipline and mutual respect within the team.  
**Action:** I introduced meeting minutes, rotated task ownership, and encouraged punctual communication and accountability.  
**Result:** The team’s submission rate improved to 100% on time deliveries. Peers highlighted my leadership and professional conduct as key contributors to the team’s success.
