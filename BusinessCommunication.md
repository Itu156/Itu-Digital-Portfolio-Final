## 💬 Reflection — STAR
**Situation:** During our Gadgets E-commerce project we had to submit weekly progress reports to our lecturer.  
**Task:** Ensure updates were concise, professional and easy to understand for non-technical reviewers.  
**Action:** I drafted structured emails highlighting key deliverables, issues, and next steps. I also revised tone and grammar to maintain formality.  
**Result:** Feedback from the lecturer was that our communication was “clear and corporate.” This skill directly improved team coordination and stakeholder trust.
